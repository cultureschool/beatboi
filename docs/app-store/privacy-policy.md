# BEATBOI Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 19, 2026**

BEATBOI is published by Quinn Sencer. This policy explains how BEATBOI handles information.

## Information BEATBOI collects

BEATBOI does not collect, sell, or share personal information. It does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, crash-reporting, accounts, or third-party data-collection SDKs.

BEATBOI does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, health data, or photos.

## Projects and settings

Your projects, patterns, sound settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device so the app can reopen them. The app does not send this project data to Quinn Sencer or to a remote server.

If you use the app's project import or export features, the files move only through the location or sharing destination you select in iOS. You control where those files are saved or shared.

## Purchases

BEATBOI offers an optional one-time Export Pack purchase through Apple's App Store and StoreKit. Apple processes the transaction. BEATBOI receives purchase entitlement information needed to unlock the feature, but does not receive or store your payment-card information.

For Apple's handling of purchase information, see Apple's privacy policy at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

## Children's privacy

BEATBOI does not knowingly collect information from children or anyone else.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new effective date.

## Contact

For support or privacy questions, open an issue at https://github.com/cultureschool/beatboi/issues.
